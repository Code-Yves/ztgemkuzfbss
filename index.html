    Faneuil Hall

    3 min walkMap

    Free. The National Park Service visitor center on the first floor is open 10am to 5pm on Sundays, with free Freedom Trail maps and rangers to ask.

    Upstairs, the Great Hall has hosted public meetings and debates for over 275 years. Open 10am to 4:30pm, unless a city event closes it.

    Photo: Daderot, CC BY-SA 3.0

    Quincy Market

    5 min walkMap

    A long food hall with 25+ local eateries, right behind Faneuil Hall. Street performers play outside daily from 11am.

    Marketplace hours Sun 11am to 7pm. Quincy Market turns 200 this year.

    The food here is not checked for dairy-free, gluten-free and soy-free. Treat it as a look-around stop.

    Photo: Upstateherd, CC BY-SA 4.0

    Waterfront: Long Wharf and Harborwalk

    10 min walkMap

    Free and open all the time. Walk out to the end of Long Wharf for harbor and boat views.

    Christopher Columbus Park is right next to it, a good place to sit. Follow the Harborwalk along the water to the Aquarium.

    Photo: Judyg143 on Pixabay, CC0

    New England Aquarium Optional

    11 min walkMap

    Timed tickets. Adults $39.95, teens pay adult. Weekends and holidays often sell out. Not booked.

    Weekend fall hours 9am to 6pm. Allow about 2 hr. Sea lion presentation 2pm and penguin presentation 2:30pm (schedule can change).

    Food inside is not checked for dairy, gluten and soy.

    Photo: 4300streetcar, CC BY 4.0

    North End

    4 min walkMap

    One of Boston's oldest neighborhoods. Narrow streets, Italian bakeries and cafes along Hanover Street. Free to wander.

    Bakery treats like cannoli have dairy and gluten. Skip them. Terramia, your 5pm dinner, is here on Salem Street.

    Photo: Tim Sackton from Somerville, MA, CC BY-SA 2.0

    Paul Revere House

    6 min walkMap

    Built around 1680. Paul Revere lived here when he made his famous ride.

    Open daily 10am to 5:15pm through Oct 31. Adults $6, ages 5 to 17 $1. A short visit, about 20 to 30 min.

    Photo: Rob Young from United Kingdom, CC BY 2.0

    Old North Church

    8 min walkMap

    Where two lanterns were hung in the steeple on April 18, 1775. Sunday visiting 12:30pm to 5pm, after the 11am service.

    Discovery Pass $10 adults, $5 youth, with the crypt and a family scavenger hunt. Bell chamber tours Sun 2pm, 3pm and 4:30pm ($15 adults, $10 youth, steep stairs).

    On Salem Street, about 5 min from Terramia. A good last stop before dinner.

    Photo: LEvalyn, CC BY 4.0
